# `mesh/` — Tier 2: fabric

Routing and registration: the `mesh-hub` daemon, the port protocol's wire types with their golden fixtures, the hub runtime the daemon wraps, the Rust client, the envelope basket, and the broadcast, health and schema registries. The vocabulary the fabric registers against (the Lexicon, the context and capability registries) is the standard's, under its `pulse/` tier, and the `register_stages!` macro the rings compose with is the standard's too, under `core/`. The book describes this tier in [Fabric: mesh/](../book/src/architecture/mesh.md), the daemon in [Daemon design](../book/src/explanation/daemon-design.md) and its flags in [The mesh-hub daemon](../book/src/reference/daemon-reference.md), and the port protocol in [chapter 7](../book/src/reference/spec/07-port-protocol.md).

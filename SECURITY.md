# Security

Pulse Mesh 1.0.0-beta is a research preview released under the Sovereignty Foundation Research License, Version 1.3. This policy covers this repository and the other two Pulse Mesh repositories.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report a vulnerability privately to **org@sovfound.org**, with "Security: Pulse Mesh" in the subject. Do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion about it.

Include what you can of:

- the repository, version and commit;
- the component and the configuration affected;
- the steps that reproduce it, and what an attacker gains;
- whether you know of it being exploited.

Do not send credentials, other people's personal data or exploit code against systems you do not own.

## Disclosure

Section 6.2 of the license asks that you report vulnerabilities, harmful failure modes, active exploitation or misuse to the address above, and that you give the Foundation a reasonable chance to fix a vulnerability before you publish it. Reporting is voluntary, and the license puts no other condition on publishing what you find.
